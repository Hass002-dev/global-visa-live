Different rules for some nationalities

  • Free: children under 6, students and accompanying teachers on educational trips, third-country researchers travelling for scientific research, under-25 non-profit representatives at seminars or events, diplomatic/official/service passport holders with a courtesy visa, and family members of EU citizens (ESC code).
  • Spain publishes its visa fee in GBP, not EUR.

Important

Before you apply

  • Spain's funds requirement is far higher than Greece's: GBP 107 per day with a GBP 960 floor, against GBP 40 per day and no floor.
  • For business applications every document needs sworn Spanish translation and legalisation, which is a real cost to warn clients about.
