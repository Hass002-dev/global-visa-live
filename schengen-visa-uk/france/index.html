Appointments

How appointments work

For France, you do not pick a slot. You register, and an appointment is allocated to you automatically once one becomes available.

Short-stay applicants are placed in a queue and notified when an appointment becomes available. They then have 24 hours to pay the TLScontact service fee and confirm the appointment. All other visa categories book directly and must pay within 30 minutes.

Important

Before you apply

  • INDICATIVE LIST. France publishes no public checklist, so this must be shown with the disclaimer and never presented as definitive.
  • The attestation d'accueil must be the original paper document posted from France. The host attends their Mairie in person and pays for it, so allow weeks rather than days.
  • Confirmed from official sources: two photographs, and a UK residence permit valid 1 month beyond return.
  • The EUR 65 per day and EUR 32.50 per day tiers come from unofficial sources. Present them as a guide, not a rule.
