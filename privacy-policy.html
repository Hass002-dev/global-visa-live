2. WhatsApp and Our AI Assistant

When you message us on WhatsApp, our first replies may come from an automated (AI) assistant rather than a person. We use an automated assistant provided by Blueticks (Serenix Ltd, Cyprus). It answers common questions, takes a few basic details about your enquiry, such as your name and the country you want to go to, and leaves a message for our team. A member of our team then replies to you personally in the same WhatsApp conversation. Blueticks, and the AI technology providers it works with, process your messages to do this, mainly in the United States. WhatsApp (Meta) is the messaging channel your chat travels over. The assistant works inside WhatsApp Web and reads the messages in the chats it handles. It does not handle anything else: calls, pictures, documents and payments are dealt with by our team personally.

You can ask for a person at any time. Ask for a member of our team on WhatsApp, or email us at office@globalvisajourneys.com.

Please do not send passport copies, bank statements or other sensitive documents in the WhatsApp chat. The assistant does not ask for or handle documents. If you do send a document in the chat, it goes to our team in our own WhatsApp account, which we manage ourselves. Our team will tell you how to send documents securely, for example through our client portal.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data to:

  • Assess your visa readiness and provide an initial consultation
  • Prepare, review, and manage your visa application case, where you have engaged our services
  • Arrange flight reservations where requested
  • Communicate with you about your case via WhatsApp, email, or our client portal
  • Answer your first WhatsApp messages using our AI assistant, and hand the conversation to a member of our team (see Section 2)
  • Send you our newsletter, if you have subscribed
  • Run our referral programme
  • Keep our website and forms secure and free of spam
  • Respond to enquiries made via live chat or other contact methods
  • Understand how our website is used, so we can improve it

4. Our Legal Basis for Processing Your Data

We rely on the following legal bases under UK GDPR:

  • Contract: where processing is necessary to provide the service you have engaged us for (e.g., managing your visa case)
  • Consent: where you have actively provided information to us (e.g., via the readiness check, live chat, or flight reservation form)
  • Legitimate interests: for keeping our website and forms secure and improving our services, balanced against your right to privacy
  • Consent (cookies and marketing): for analytics and live-chat cookies, which we set only if you click “Accept All”, and for marketing messages such as our newsletter. We only send marketing with your consent, and we do not use your WhatsApp enquiry to send you marketing unless you agree
  • WhatsApp enquiries, including those handled by our AI assistant: taking steps at your request before a contract (Article 6(1)(b) UK GDPR), where you ask us about one of our services
  • Sharing your information with visa application centres: taking steps at your request and performing our contract with you (Article 6(1)(b) UK GDPR), because applications can only be lodged through those centres

Where your documents include sensitive information (such as financial records for visa purposes), we only process this with your clear, informed consent, and only for the specific purpose of your visa case.

When we help with a visa application, we share your information with the visa application centre that handles applications for your country (for example VFS Global, BLS International, TLScontact, Capago or GVCW, whichever applies) because applications can only be lodged through those centres. Your application and supporting documents are submitted to the embassy or consulate of the country you are applying to, through the centre, and it is that embassy or consulate that decides the application. We do this only on your instructions, as part of the service you have asked us to provide, and we pass on only what is needed: your identity and contact details, your passport details, your travel dates and purpose, appointment booking details (where we book an appointment for you), and the application form and supporting documents you give us.

Once we pass your information on, those organisations are independent controllers of it. They handle it under their own privacy notices, and we are not responsible for how they handle it. They may be located outside the UK; see Section 6 (International Data Transfers).

6. International Data Transfers

Some of our third-party service providers (including Google) may process data on servers located outside the UK. Where this occurs, the safeguards come from the contracts our providers have in place, such as Standard Contractual Clauses, to protect your data to UK GDPR standards. This may apply to Google, Cloudflare, Meta (WhatsApp), Mailchimp (Intuit), Stripe, Tawk.to, Blueticks and the AI technology providers it works with, and the visa application centres, embassies and consulates described in Section 5.

Blueticks is based in Cyprus, and transfers of data from the UK to the European Economic Area are covered by UK adequacy regulations. Blueticks and its providers process data mainly in the United States, including on Google Cloud and Amazon Web Services. Blueticks’ data processing agreement says that onward transfers to sub-processors outside the EEA use the EU Standard Contractual Clauses together with, for UK data, the UK Addendum, the International Data Transfer Agreement, or the UK–US data bridge where the provider is certified. Blueticks publishes its list of sub-processors at blueticks.co/dpa.

7. How Long We Keep Your Data

We keep your personal data for these periods:

  • Enquiries that do not become clients, including WhatsApp chats: deleted after 12 months
  • Your client case file (your engagement letter, our correspondence with you, and the case record): kept for the length of the engagement plus 6 years
  • Financial and invoice records: kept for 6 years from the end of the accounting period, as HMRC requires
  • Passport scans and bank statements: deleted 12 months after your case closes. We keep only the correspondence and the engagement record
  • You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 8 below). We will delete it unless the law requires us to keep it, for example our financial records

8. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request erasure of your data (“right to be forgotten”)
  • Restrict how we process your data in certain circumstances
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Data portability — receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent
  • Ask for a person to review any decision made about you solely by automated means, where that decision has a legal or similarly significant effect on you (see Section 2)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at office@globalvisajourneys.com. We will respond within one month, as required by law.

You also have the right to complain to the UK’s data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), at ico.org.uk, if you believe we have not handled your data properly.

9. Cookies

Our website uses cookies from the following services, and only if you click “Accept All” on our cookie banner:

  • Google Analytics — to understand how visitors use our site
  • Tawk.to — to enable our live chat function

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of live chat or analytics, but will not prevent you from using the core features of our website.

We save your cookie choice on your device, in your browser’s local storage, so that we do not ask you again. If you sign in to our client portal, your sign-in is also kept on your device so that you stay logged in; this is strictly necessary for the portal to work.

Our pages also load fonts, icons and images from Google Fonts, cdnjs (operated by Cloudflare) and Unsplash. When a page loads, your IP address and browser details are sent to those providers so that they can deliver that content.

10. Data Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data, including password-protected access to our client portal, secure cloud storage with access controls, and restricted internal access to case documents on a need-to-know basis. Blueticks’ data processing agreement commits it to tell us within 72 hours if it becomes aware of a personal data breach affecting our data.

11. Children’s Privacy

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18 without appropriate parental or guardian consent, as may be required for certain visa applications involving minors.
