Schengen Visa from the UK: Spain vs Greece — Which Is Easier to Get in 2026?

GV&J Strategy Team 12 May 2026 3 min read

For UK residents who need a Schengen visa — typically Nigerians, Indians, Pakistanis, and other non-EU passport holders living in the UK — the question every season is the same: which country is easiest to apply through?

For 2026, two routes consistently outperform the rest: Spain and Greece. Both have higher approval rates, shorter waiting times, and more reasonable financial thresholds than France, Germany, or Italy. But they are not equally easy. Here is the direct comparison.

Appointment Availability

This is where most applications die before they even begin. You cannot apply without an appointment, and the appointment portals are notoriously gridlocked.

Spain (BLS International — London) Spain reopened its London centres in mid-2024 after a long period of difficulty. As of early 2026, appointments are typically available within 2 to 4 weeks during off-peak months, stretching to 6 to 8 weeks in summer. Tier-list booking is now in effect — premium fast-track slots are available at extra cost.

Greece (BLS International — London) Greece has historically been the easier route. Appointments are usually available within 1 to 3 weeks year-round. There is no premium tier needed — the standard slot is the fast slot. This is the single biggest advantage Greece holds.

Verdict: Greece wins on appointments by a clear margin.

Approval Rates

Approval data is not officially published per consulate, but our internal data from London applications over the past 18 months gives a reliable picture.

  • Spain: ~92% approval rate for properly prepared cases. Higher scrutiny on tourism applications without family ties in Spain.
  • Greece: ~94% approval rate for properly prepared cases. Slightly more lenient on first-time travellers.

Both numbers are excellent. The differentiator is preparation, not the country.

Verdict: Marginal advantage to Greece, but the gap is small.

Financial Thresholds (2026)

These are the official daily minimum amounts you must demonstrate.

Country Daily Rate 7-Day Trip 14-Day Trip
Spain €100/day, €900 minimum £774 £1,204
Greece €50/day, €300 minimum £301 £602

The Greek thresholds are roughly half what Spain requires. For applicants on more modest budgets, Greece is significantly more accessible. Note that Spain's €900 minimum applies regardless of how short the trip is.

Verdict: Greece wins decisively on financial thresholds.

Processing Time

Once your file is submitted, how long until decision?

  • Spain: 7 to 15 working days for standard processing. Premium expedited service can return decisions in 3 to 5 working days at additional cost.
  • Greece: 10 to 15 working days standard. No premium expedited tier currently offered for London.

Verdict: Spain has the edge if you need an emergency turnaround. Otherwise broadly equal.

Documentation Stringency

Both countries require the same core documents — passport, bank statements, employment letter, accommodation, travel insurance, itinerary. The differences are in what triggers a flag.

Spain scrutinises:

  • Source of funds for any deposit over €3,000 in the last 90 days
  • Accommodation booking authenticity — they sometimes verify with the hotel
  • Return ticket evidence — preferring confirmed bookings over reservations

Greece scrutinises:

  • 6-month bank statement history — they want continuity, not snapshots
  • Employment letter wording — they expect specific dates of approved leave
  • Whether you've travelled before — first-time travellers get harder scrutiny

Both countries accept verified flight reservations (dummy tickets) provided they're from a legitimate provider. Neither requires a fully paid ticket before approval.

When to Choose Spain

Choose Spain if:

  • You're a higher earner with comfortable savings (£1,500+ accessible)
  • You can afford the premium fast-track if needed
  • Your trip is genuinely focused on Spanish destinations
  • You have a history of approved Schengen visas

When to Choose Greece

Choose Greece if:

  • You're on a more modest budget (£600–£1,500 accessible)
  • This is your first Schengen application from the UK
  • Your appointment timeline is tight
  • You want the highest probability of approval first try

The Strategic Move

Here is what most agents won't tell you: Schengen visas issued by any member state allow you to travel within the entire 27-country area, provided your stay in the issuing country represents the majority or first port of entry.

This means if you obtain a Greek visa, you can still visit Paris, Barcelona, Amsterdam, Berlin — as long as Greece is your main destination or first entry point. Many of our UK clients use Greece as their "gateway country" for exactly this reason: easier visa, full Schengen access.
