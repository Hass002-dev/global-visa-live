Important Notes

All fees quoted are for GVJ's professional service. This may include the administrative appointment-reservation fee charged by VFS, BLS, or TLS, which GVJ books and pays on the Client's behalf as part of the service. It does not include the core embassy or consulate visa fee itself, travel insurance, or accommodation bookings, which remain the Client's direct responsibility.
Where a fee is quoted as a range, a fixed price will always be confirmed in writing before work begins. No client is ever charged more than the fixed quote they agreed to.
GVJ does not guarantee visa approval under any circumstances. All fees relate to the quality and thoroughness of our service, not to any outcome of a visa application, which rests solely with the relevant government authority.
